Profile

Yuto Motohashi is a researcher working in the field of quantum engineering.
He holds a Bachelor in Applied Physics, Engineering from The University of Tokyo
He is currently a Master of Science (MSc) student in Quantum Engineering at ETH Zurich (starting September 2024).
Beginning in January 2026, he will conduct his master’s thesis at Stanford University as a visiting student researcher.
He is supported by the Funai Overseas Scholarship (link) and is interested in trapped-ion quantum computers, working on experimental research.
